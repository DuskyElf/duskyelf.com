Implies is a logic gate, but is often misunderstood.

| $A$ | $B$ | $A \implies B$ |
|---|---|---|
| T | T | T |
| T | F | F |
| F | T | T |
| F | F | T |

There's no causality, there's no time, it's just the raw truth table.

$A \implies B$ is not equivalent to $\lnot A \implies \lnot B$

"I think therefore I am" does not mean "I don't think therefore I do not am"

## Contrapositive
$\lnot B \implies \lnot A$

$(A \implies B) \implies (B \lor \lnot A)$

"Raining implies I take my umbrella, if I don't have an umbrella then it better not be raining"
